I’m a passionate full-stack developer combining technical craftsmanship and creative vision. My background as an archaeologist has shaped the way I approach technology: working in complex, intercultural environments taught me precision, resilience, and a fascination for uncovering hidden structures, skills I now channel into building digital systems. Reflectance Transformation Imaging (RTI) Toolset

An open-source toolset for Reflectance Transformation Imaging, a technique used in cultural heritage to capture and reveal fine surface detail. It spans the full pipeline, from preparing raw captures to rendering them interactively in the browser.
