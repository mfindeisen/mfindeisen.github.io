About Me

I’m a passionate full-stack developer combining technical craftsmanship and creative vision. I specialize in immersive web experiences, 3D visualisation (Three.js/WebGL), and bridging UI/UX with performant, maintainable backends. My background as an archaeologist has shaped the way I approach technology: working in complex, intercultural environments taught me precision, resilience, and a fascination for uncovering hidden structures—skills I now channel into building digital systems. Beyond development, I’m driven by curiosity for geospatial technologies, creative coding, and exploring how storytelling, data, and design can merge into meaningful interactive experiences.
